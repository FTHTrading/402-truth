Truth labels

A label says how a statement came to be, never whether it is right. OBSERVED the issuer measured it · ATTESTED a named signer asserted it · VERIFIED a deterministic check passed · CONFIRMED an external system corroborated it · PENDING · REFUSED with reason codes · PROJECTION nothing was executed. Anchored, revoked, superseded and expired are derived by the verifier, never asserted at issuance.
